# Paper Manuscript Writing

This package is for manuscript drafting, restructuring, polishing, de-AI rewriting, and thesis writing guidance.

Its primary role is writing work on paper or thesis content. The bundled scripts in `scripts/` are attached support tooling for thesis-oriented import/export, template parsing, dependency checks, and version-management workflows.

Final DOCX delivery is shared with `paper-docx-repair`: export from this package, then render the resulting DOCX to page PNGs and verify every page before archive or handoff.

Out of scope:

- literature collection, reading, and synthesis workflows
- reviewer-response workflows
- presentation or defense slide workflows

Canonical worked examples live under `references/examples/`.
