# Example output: journal club slide outline

- Paper title
- Authors and venue
- One-sentence claim

- Problem statement
- Prior limitation
- Why the audience should care

## Slide 3 - Study design

- Core method or experimental setup
- Data source or cohort
- Key comparison

- Primary finding
- Figure cue: `Figure 2`

## Slide 5 - Limitations and open questions

- What the paper does not resolve
- Reviewer-style caveat or replication concern

## Slide 6 - Discussion prompt

- One question for the audience
- One practical implication
